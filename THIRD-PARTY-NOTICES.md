# Third-party resources and source terms

- Leaflet 1.9.4: BSD 2-Clause. Original license is included at dist/vendor/LICENSE-Leaflet.txt (in the source package, under work/somalia-flood-watch/). JS/CSS and standard image assets are bundled. Source: https://github.com/Leaflet/Leaflet/tree/v1.9.4
- Natural Earth country polygons and river centerlines: public-domain reference geography. Generated regional geometry is bundled; the source ZIP includes the GeoJSON build inputs. Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- The logo and interface icons are code-native SVG in the application. No external font or icon account is needed.
- IBM Plex Sans (regular, semibold, bold; Latin subset) by IBM: SIL Open Font License 1.1. The WOFF2 files are bundled at dist/fonts/ and served by the app itself, so no font is fetched from another site. The license is included at dist/fonts/LICENSE-IBM-Plex.txt (in the source package, under work/somalia-flood-watch/). Files from the Fontsource package @fontsource/ibm-plex-sans 5.1.1. Source: https://github.com/IBM/plex
- FFmpeg (container image only): installed from the Alpine Linux package when the image is built, to convert the audio bulletins to MP3. It is not part of this repository. FFmpeg is licensed under the LGPL 2.1 or later, with some parts under the GPL; source code: https://ffmpeg.org and https://pkgs.alpinelinux.org/packages?name=ffmpeg
- Audio bulletins are voiced by Google's Gemini text-to-speech service under Google's API terms; the scripts are the site's own, written from the sources above.
- Weather, river models, alerts, terrain services and satellite products remain provider services with their own terms; public access does not mean every source is public domain. Existing attribution and independent-service notices are retained in the app and app guide. Community media keep contributors' rights.
- Developer-only test fixtures are dated parser examples, not live advisories or operational measurements. The SWALIM HTML fixture is a compact reconstruction of the published factual station table, without the source site's scripts or page design. The ICPAC fixtures are dated responses from the public East Africa Hazards Watch dataset list and map server (forecast issued 13 October 2026). WMS/WMTS XML fixtures describe public service metadata. They are never shown as current community reports.

This file records third-party terms; it does not assign a new licence to the custom application code.
